INGOVIZ AI

AI-powered business intelligence for credit analysis

Drop in a company’s financials, GST returns and bank statements, as files, a folder or a ZIP. INGOVIZ AI reads them, reconciles one against another and gives a lender a complete credit view in minutes.

  • Reconciles financials, GST and banking turnover, and flags gaps month by month
  • Credit score across 13 dimensions with a GO / CONDITIONAL GO / NO-GO call
  • Automatic SWOT where every point shows its ratio, source and Low / Medium / High severity
  • Built-in assistant that answers questions about the loaded data
  • Exports a 40-section credit report PDF, Excel, HTML and a ready Power BI project

TeamLead by INGOVIQ AI

An AI team leader for small teams

TeamLead plans every person’s day at 9:30, checks the real work as it happens and scores the day at 7 PM. Each person gets clear feedback; the manager gets one summary, and an alert when someone needs help.

  • AI daily plans with times and targets for each person’s role
  • New leads shared out fairly; follow-ups come back on time
  • Work checked against the real lead list, not typed-in updates
  • A daily score out of 100 with two lines of feedback for each person
  • Skips weekends, alternate Saturdays and holidays
